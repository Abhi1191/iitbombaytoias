About IIT Bombay To IAS

Building the Future of AI-Powered Personalized Learning for Competitive Examinations

Our Story

IIT Bombay To IAS started with a simple vision—making high-quality competitive examination preparation affordable, structured, and accessible to every serious aspirant. The platform currently provides classroom-oriented courses, PYQ discussions, test series, mentorship, and exam-oriented guidance for UPSC and BPSC aspirants.

Our long-term vision is to evolve beyond a traditional EdTech platform into an AI-powered personalized learning ecosystem where every student receives intelligent guidance tailored to their preparation journey.

Our Mission

To build India's most intelligent AI-powered learning platform that combines technology, mentorship, and data-driven insights to help every competitive examination aspirant prepare more effectively.

Our Vision

  • Build India's AI Personal Mentor for Competitive Exams
  • Deliver Personalized Learning instead of One-Size-Fits-All Coaching
  • Combine AI + Human Mentorship
  • Improve Learning Outcomes using Data Intelligence
  • Expand from UPSC & State PCS to SSC, Banking, Railways, Defence, IIT-JEE, NEET, GATE and CAT
